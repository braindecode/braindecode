`seanet.py` module holds its SEANet codec, derived from Meta's

residual vector quantization derived from `lucidrains/vector-quantize-pytorch`
(MIT) and the BrainOmni port.
The `linear_attention.py` module is the part of
`lucidrains/linear-attention-transformer` (MIT) that BIOT and TFMTokenizer run.
The `tccnet.py` file is a derivative of `DSIP-UPatras/ICASSP2019_TCN` (MIT),
whose average-over-time and context-attention layers come from
`cbaziotis/keras-utilities` (MIT).
